Four global fixes (do these once, everything tightens)

1 · Pick ONE labelling system

Three are running at once and colliding: the pathways I / II / III — Beyond Crisis / Camden / Price; the brief’s C1–C5; and III.1 / III.2 on the taste slides — plus the stale “Beyond Health.” Choose the pathways as the only public labels, and if you want progression inside a pathway, use a consistent “I·1, I·2 …” counter. Retire C# and III.# from the slides.

2 · Cut / merge the duplicates & placeholders (≈12 slides)

  • Exact/near duplicates: 10 (=9) · 40 (≈39) · 43 (≈42) · 64 & 65 (≈63) · 87 (=86) · 112 (=111) · 49 (≈48)
  • Empty / placeholder: 21 · 93 · 6 (“Lorem ipsum”) · 33 (working note) · 94 (“Sodertajle??”) · the “Lorem” tags littering 98–105

3 · Fix the mis-tags

Beyond Price slides 106 / 108 / 109 say “II” → should be “III.” The premature I/II tags in Act 1 (16–20, 23–24) → drop, that act is shared context.

4 · Decide the home of the demand-side / taste material (Act 6)

It’s the single biggest structural question. My recommendation: make it an explicit cross-cutting interlude titled something like “The Demand Side — the resilient plate,” placed right after the three pathways are introduced (Act 3) or as a clearly-marked bridge before Beyond Price — not left unlabelled between II and III where it reads as a missing fourth pathway.

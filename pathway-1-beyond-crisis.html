Beyond Crisis

Universal Nutrition Infrastructure

Camden has built a sophisticated system for managing food poverty — food banks, mutual aid, redistribution, emergency protocols. It works, and it is a sign of profound structural failure. Food banks are a symptom, not a solution. The task is to build the permanent civic infrastructure that makes them unnecessary.

03 — The shift

From managing crisis to building infrastructure

The move is not to run the safety net better — it is to make the safety net progressively unnecessary by building permanent, dignified, universal nutrition infrastructure.

Today · Crisis management

Emergency provision

  • Charity-model food banks & surplus redistribution
  • Reactive, means-tested, undignified
  • 3–4× the direct cost of the food
  • Dependency-building, never resolving
→
2040 · Universal infrastructure

Built-in nutrition

  • Community-owned food hub & cold chain
  • Borough-wide cooperative supply
  • Food bank → dignified member-led pantry
  • Universal Basic Nutrition as a civic floor

05 — Where it acts
The inner rings

Pathway I works at the most intimate scales — the household, the estate, the neighbourhood, the borough. Universal nutrition is measured one home at a time: does this family have dignified access to good food, built into the place they live? It is the innermost layer of the food-system onion — the ground the other pathways reach outward from.

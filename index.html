II
Beyond Camden
Regional Food Sovereignty
▶
Camden is 22km². It has almost no agricultural land. Every vegetable, every loaf, every piece of meat enters from outside — through three or four national chokepoints. The productive capacity to feed Camden exists within 50 miles. The relationship does not. Beyond Camden means building that relationship.

III
Beyond Price
New Food Economics
▶
Food is cheap. That is the problem. Its costs are paid elsewhere — by the NHS treating diet-related disease, by local authorities funding emergency provision, by farm workers on poverty wages. Camden is already paying for this. Just at the wrong end.
